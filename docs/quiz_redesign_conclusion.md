# Quiz Redesign: Integrated Profiling & Narrative Ideology Matching

## 1. Objective:
*   To guide users towards a 'better alternative' political party.
*   To provide a highly engaging, personalized, and low-maintenance quiz experience.
*   To offer actionable recommendations relevant to the upcoming election cycle.

## 2. Core Components:
*   **Initial Profiling Step:** A non-ideological pre-quiz to gather basic user context (e.g., renter/homeowner, parent/non-parent, urban/rural) based on existing quiz.
*   **Dynamic Question Selection:** Narrative questions will be dynamically selected from a larger bank based on the user's profile, ensuring relevance.
*   **Narrative Ideology Quiz:** The main quiz will consist of 7-8 'story-based' questions presented in a head-to-head 'this or that' format. Each question will be a mini-narrative asking the user to solve a relatable problem, thereby revealing their preference on an underlying ideological axis (e.g., Market vs. State, Individual vs. Collective).
*   **Ideological Matching Algorithm:** The backend will map user choices to an ideological profile and identify the party with the closest 'worldview' or problem-solving approach.
*   **Actionable Results Presentation:** The final recommendation will connect the user's abstract ideological alignment back to concrete, near-term issues, framing the party as the best 'service provider' for their priorities in the next election.

## 3. Key Design Principles:
*   **Low Mental Load:** The quiz interaction is designed to be effortless and intuitive (e.g., 'this or that' taps).
*   **High Engagement:** Narrative questions and a game-like interaction (e.g., tournament bracket logic) will keep users engaged.
*   **Personalization:** Questions are tailored to the user's specific needs and context.
*   **Timelessness:** Matching is based on core ideological approaches rather than transient party promises, ensuring long-term relevance.
*   **Actionability:** Results are framed to empower the user with a clear understanding of how their vote can lead to tangible improvements.
